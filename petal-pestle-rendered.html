One lesson, in the order the engine walks it

hb-m1-01 · Inside a seed · 45 minutes

Phase 1 · Check-in

The snack bowl mystery

Pour out popcorn kernels, dried beans, rice and sunflower seeds. Every one of these is a seed. Every one could be a plant.

Think about it

So why are they sitting in a bowl instead of growing?

Phase 2 · Teach — beat 1 of 2

Did you know?

Every seed on earth is built from the same three parts — an acorn, a grain of rice, and the corn in your bucket.

What a seed is made of

A seed has a hard skin called the seed coat. Inside is a tiny baby plant, the embryo. Packed in beside it is its food, the endosperm.

Worked example

Split a soaked bean and you can see all three with your eyes. The skin peels off. The little white curl is the baby. The two fat halves are its lunch.

Try it

Your bean grew in a bag with a wet paper towel and no dirt at all. What fed it?

  1. The paper towel
  2. Its own endosperm
  3. The water
  4. Nothing — it was not really growing

The lunch is packed inside the seed. It lasts until leaves can make food from sunlight.

Phase 3 · Teach — beat 2 of 2

Did you know?

Some seeds have woken up after sleeping for more than a thousand years.

How a seed wakes up

A seed in a bowl is dormant. That means alive but asleep. Give it water, warmth and the right light and the coat cracks open. Waking up is called germination.

Worked example

The root always comes out first, and it always grows down — even if the seed was lying upside down. The shoot goes up second.

Try it

You plant a bean upside down by mistake. What does the root do?

  1. Grows up, the wrong way
  2. Grows down anyway
  3. Stops growing
  4. Turns into a shoot

A seed senses gravity. However you plant it, the root heads down and the shoot heads up.

Phase 4 · Activity — away from the screen

Open a seed, then wake four up

Get ready first

Soak six dried beans in water overnight. A dry bean will not open. Lima or pinto are easiest.

What you need

  • 6 dried beans, soaked
  • a bowl of dry seeds you can eat
  • 4 zip bags
  • 4 paper towels
  • water
  • tape
  1. Split a soaked bean along its seam with your thumbnail.
  2. Peel the seed coat off first. Find all three parts.
  3. Find the tiny curl. That is the embryo. The baby plant.
  4. The two big fat halves are the endosperm. Its lunch.
  5. Bag 1: a bean in a WET paper towel. Tape it to a sunny window.
  6. Bag 2: a DRY paper towel. Sunny window. No water.
  7. Bag 3: a wet paper towel. A dark closet. No light.
  8. Bag 4: a wet paper towel. The fridge. No warmth.
  9. Guess which bags will germinate. Write it down before you know.
  10. Check once a day for two weeks. Watch which part comes out first.

Safety

The bowl of dry seeds is a snack. The soaked beans are NOT. Never taste a plant without a grown-up.

Phase 5 · Ledger — what gets written down

  • Label your split bean: seed coat, embryo, endosperm.
  • Circle Y or N for each bag. Your guess, before Day 1.
  • Which part came out first? Write the day it happened.
  • Write one question about seeds for Gigi. You must know the answer.

Word Boss

SEED COAT · EMBRYO · ENDOSPERM · DORMANT · GERMINATION

  1. Say what each word means in your own words. No fancy talk.
  2. A grown-up says the meaning. You say the word. Beat the clock.
  3. Stump Gigi. Write her one question you already know the answer to.

If she is stuck · Point at the split bean on the table instead of saying the answer. The word is easier when the thing is in front of her.

Nothing here is graded. Nothing is corrected. Petals are for doing it.

Phase 6 · Talk it through — never marked

What are the three parts of every seed?

The seed coat, the embryo and the endosperm.

A coat to keep it safe, a baby plant, and a lunch to get it started.

A seed sits in a jar for two years. Is it alive?

Yes. It is dormant.

Dormant means asleep, not dead. Some seeds wait far longer than two years.

Phase 7 · The check — this one is graded

1. Which part of a seed is the baby plant?

  1. The embryo
  2. The seed coat
  3. The endosperm
  4. The root

2. Popcorn kernels sit in a bowl and never grow. Why not?

  1. They are dead
  2. They are dormant
  3. They are not really seeds
  4. They have no embryo

3. When a seed germinates, which part comes out first?

  1. The leaves
  2. The flower
  3. The root
  4. The shoot
