LEARNINGOS - how to open it
===========================

Go to:   https://mission-control-homeschool.netlify.app

That is the whole thing. Bookmark it on both computers.

There is no black window any more, and nothing to install.


SIGNING IN  (this part is new)
------------------------------
The app now asks who is at the keyboard.

  Students   - your name, then your four numbers.
  Grown-ups  - the Parent Login, with your passcode.

The computer remembers whoever signed in last, so most mornings
you will land straight in school without being asked anything.
You only see the sign-in page on a new computer, after Sign Out,
or the very first time.

FORGOT THE PASSCODE? On the Parent Login there is a link that
says "Forgot it - use my recovery code". That is the code you
were shown once at setup and asked to write down. It is the only
way back in - there is no server anywhere to email a reset link.
Keep it with the homeschool records.

FORGOT THE FOUR NUMBERS? Ask your grown-up. She can set new ones.


USE THE SAME BROWSER EVERY DAY
------------------------------
Your work is saved inside the browser, on the computer you are
sitting at - not on the website. So:

  * Same browser, every day. Chrome work does not show up in Edge.
  * Not a private / incognito window. Those forget everything the
    moment you close them.

If the app ever opens completely empty, that is almost always the
wrong browser or a private window. Nothing has been lost. Close it,
open your normal browser, and go to the address again.


YOU STILL HAVE TO TRADE FILES
-----------------------------
This is the part the website does NOT do for you.

Both computers show the same app. They do NOT share the same
records. Grades, attendance and Khan ticks live on the computer
they were earned on until they are sent.

  Every morning  - load Mom's file  ("Get my graded work back")
  End of the day - send yours       ("Send my work to Mom")

Both buttons are on the Morning Meeting screen. Loading the same
file twice is safe - the second time does nothing.

Skip a day and that day's attendance never reaches the record.


WHEN MOM CHANGES THE APP
------------------------
Nothing to send, nothing to unzip. She commits in GitHub Desktop
and pushes; the site rebuilds itself in a minute or two.

If the app has been open all day, it may still be running the
older version. Reloading the page picks up the new one. The build
date in the top bar is what the tab is currently running.


IF THE PAGE WILL NOT LOAD
-------------------------
The website needs an internet connection. If the internet is down,
open the app from this folder instead - double-click

    START-LEARNINGOS.bat

That runs a local copy on this computer. Be aware that the local
copy keeps its OWN separate records, so anything done there has to
be exported and loaded back into the website copy afterwards.
Use it only when the internet is out.
