# State compliance — first verified batch

**Researched Aug 30, 2026.** Six states. Every field carries a source; anything that
could not be sourced says `UNVERIFIED` rather than being filled in.

**This is research, not legal advice.** Every entry cites what a source says,
attributed. Before shipping any state as a default, a human should read the primary

## Two corrections to widely repeated wrong information

**⚠ Tennessee does NOT require a bachelor's degree for grades 9–12.** Many secondary
sources — including several that surfaced in this research — still say it does.
Subdivision (b)(7) was **deleted by the 2023 amendment**, and TDOE's June 2026 document
states only "high school diploma or equivalency credential." Do not seed that rule.

**⚠ Georgia's statute does NOT require a monthly attendance report.** Older secondary
write-ups describe one to the local superintendent. No such requirement appears in the
current text of O.C.G.A. § 20-2-690. If the app fires a Georgia attendance-report
reminder, that reminder isn't supported by the statute.

## GEORGIA

**Two legal paths.** § 20-2-690 recognizes private schools (b) and home study programs
(c) separately. A family teaching its own children ordinarily uses **(c)**, below.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | **Declaration of Intent** to the **Georgia DOE** (not the district), within **30 days** of establishing, then **by September 1 annually**. Filed at apps.gadoe.org |
| **Contents** | Student names and ages, program address, local school system, statement of the 12-month school year |
| **Instructional time** | **180 days**, each **at least 4½ hours** |
| **Required subjects** | Reading, language arts, mathematics, social studies, science |
| **Assessment** | Nationally standardized test **every 3 years beginning end of 3rd grade**, administered in consultation with someone trained in norm-referenced testing. **Retained, NOT submitted** |
| **Records** | **Annual progress assessment report** written by the instructor, covering **each of the five subjects**, **retained 3 years**. Test records retained |
| **Parent qualification** | **High school diploma or state-approved HSE.** May teach only own children |
| **Penalty** | Misdemeanor, fine up to $100 |
| **Immunization** | `UNVERIFIED` — § 20-2-690 is silent; whether § 20-2-771 reaches home study is unresolved |

**Statute:** O.C.G.A. § 20-2-690(c)
**Sources:** [statute](https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-20/chapter-2/article-16/part-1/subpart-2/section-20-2-690/) *(statute)* · [filing portal](https://apps.gadoe.org/Pages/Home-Study-DOI.aspx) *(state DOE)* · [GaDOE homeschool](https://gadoe.org/parent-family-resources/home-school/) *(state DOE)*
**Verified:** 2026-08-30

**⚠ Amended five times since 2021** (2021, 2022, 2024, 2025, 2026). The July 2026
amendment touches only subsection **(b)** — private schools — leaving (c) unchanged.
**Re-verify each school year.**

**⚠ Open item:** GaDOE's own guidance document *"Georgia Home School: Information about
home study programs"* (published Aug 1, 2025, https://url.gadoe.org/intqf) exists and
cites § 20-2-690, but its PDF body could not be extracted. **Since this is your state,
read it and check it against the table above.**

## TEXAS

**Homeschools are legally private schools.** No separate homeschool statute.
*TEA v. Leeper* (Tex. 1994) placed home-taught children inside the private-school
exemption.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | **None.** TEA "does not regulate, index, monitor, approve, register, or accredit" homeschools. A district may request a written letter of assurance |
| **Instructional time** | **None identified** |
| **Required subjects** | Statute names only **good citizenship**. The commonly cited five — reading, spelling, grammar, mathematics, good citizenship — come from the *Leeper* trial-court judgment via **secondary sources**, not the statute. **Label the provenance if displayed** |
| **Assessment** | **None** |
| **Records** | **None identified** — `UNVERIFIED` as to any retention duty. "None found" is weaker than "none exists" |
| **Parent qualification** | **None identified** |

**Statute:** Tex. Educ. Code § 25.086(a)(1); *TEA v. Leeper*, 893 S.W.2d 432
**Sources:** [statute](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm) *(statute)* · [TEA, page dated 2026-08-03](https://tea.texas.gov/families-and-students/finding-school-your-child/home-schooling) *(state DOE)*
**Verified:** 2026-08-30

## FLORIDA

**Multiple options.** F.S. 1002.41 **home education program** is the independent-family
route, below. Others: private/umbrella school, and the **Personalized Education Program
(PEP)** — PEP students are *not* home education students and have separate rules.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | Written notice to the **district superintendent**, within **30 days** of establishing. Full legal names, addresses, birthdates. Superintendent **must accept and may not require anything further** |
| **Renewal** | **No annual renewal notice.** Initial notice, annual evaluation filing, and a termination notice within 30 days of completion |
| **Instructional time** | **Explicitly none** — "excluded from meeting the requirements of a school day" |
| **Required subjects** | **None specified** |
| **Assessment** | **Annual educational evaluation**, parent picks from 5 methods (certified-teacher portfolio review; nationally normed test; state assessment; licensed psychologist; mutually agreed measure). **A copy must be filed annually with the superintendent.** Failure to progress → 1 year to remediate |
| **Records** | **Portfolio**: a contemporaneous log of activities naming reading materials, plus samples of work. **Preserved 2 years**, available on **15 days' written notice** |
| **Parent qualification** | **None.** No teaching certificate required |
| **District overreach** | Districts "may not further regulate, exercise control over, or require documentation" beyond the section |
| **Evaluation deadline** | `UNVERIFIED` — statute says "annually" with **no calendar date**. Any reminder date the app fires is unsourced |
| **Immunization** | `UNVERIFIED` |

**Statute:** F.S. 1002.41 (last amended ch. 2018-134 — the most stable of the six)
**Sources:** [statute](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=1000-1099/1002/Sections/1002.41.html) *(statute)* · [FLDOE](https://www.fldoe.org/schools/school-choice/other-school-choice-options/home-edu/) *(state DOE)*
**Verified:** 2026-08-30

## NORTH CAROLINA

**Election required** between Part 1 (religious charter) and Part 2 (qualified
nonpublic). Requirements mirror each other; the choice is character, not substance.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | **Notice of Intent** to **NC Division of Non-Public Education (DNPE)**. **One per school, not per student. One-time, NOT annual.** Termination notice required on closing |
| **Filing window** | **July–April only. None in May or June, no exceptions.** Send ≥5 days before opening. Do not withdraw the child until DNPE's **written acknowledgment** arrives |
| **Instructional time** | **At least nine calendar months**, regular schedule. **No hours-per-day requirement in law** — DNPE's "five hours daily" and "daily log" are listed under **Recommendations, explicitly not mandated** |
| **Required subjects** | **None mandated** |
| **Assessment** | Nationally standardized test **every academic year, every student**, measuring **English grammar, reading, spelling, mathematics**. First test within the start year. **Records kept ≥1 year at the school**, available on request — **not routinely submitted**. High school adds an 11th-grade competency measure |
| **Records** | **Annual attendance records** and **immunization records** |
| **Parent qualification** | **High school diploma or equivalent.** Documentation **submitted with the NOI** for every instruction provider |

**Statute:** G.S. 115C-563, -564; Part 1 §§ 115C-548/549/550/552; Part 2 §§ 115C-556/557/558/560
**Sources:** [statute](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByArticle/Chapter_115C/Article_39.html) *(statute)* · [DNPE, modified 2026-08-03](https://www.doa.nc.gov/divisions/non-public-education/home-schools/requirements-recommendations) *(state agency)*
**Verified:** 2026-08-30

## TENNESSEE

**Three routes.** § 49-6-3050 covers the **independent home school** registered with
the LEA, below. The statute exempts two church-related-school routes. A fourth
route — accredited online school as school of record — is named by secondary sources
but **not in the statute**; `UNVERIFIED`.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | **Annual notice to the local director of schools before each school year** — filed with the **district**, not the state. Must report names, ages, grade levels, **location, proposed curriculum, proposed hours, and parent qualifications**. Proof of the parent's education accompanies it |
| **Instructional time** | **4 hours/day × the public-school day count** — TDOE renders this as **4 hrs × 180 days** |
| **Required subjects** | **None in statute.** Parent selects and purchases curriculum; district asks it be identified |
| **Assessment** | **Grades 5, 7, and 9.** Either a nationally normed test **proctored by a non-relative** (parent expense, within 30 days of the state test date) or the state-board test free at the local school. **Results go to the parent, the director of schools, AND the state board** |
| **Remediation** | 3–6 months behind → consultation. 6–9 months behind → licensed-teacher consult + remedial plan reported to the director. >1 year behind on two consecutive tests → director **may require enrollment** elsewhere |
| **Records** | **Attendance records**, inspectable at any time, and **submitted to the director at the end of each school year**. Parent maintains grades, transcripts, diplomas |
| **Parent qualification** | **High school diploma or state-approved HSE — all grades.** See the correction at the top |
| **Immunization** | **Not required** to register for independent home school (TDOE) |

**Statute:** T.C.A. § 49-6-3050 (amended 2023 ch. 296, 2024 ch. 658)
**Sources:** [statute](https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-49/chapter-6/part-30/section-49-6-3050/) *(statute)* · [TDOE, revised June 2026](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/education/nonpublic/Independent_Home_School_Requirements.pdf) *(state DOE)*
**Verified:** 2026-08-30

## CALIFORNIA

**No homeschool statute; five routes.** CDE names: public charter, public independent
study, existing private school / PSP, **home-based private school via the Private
School Affidavit** (below), and credentialed private tutor.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | **Private School Affidavit**, filed **annually** with the **Superintendent of Public Instruction / CDE** — not the district. Statutory window **Oct 1–15**; CDE keeps the system open **Aug 1 – Jun 30**. Filed **under penalty of perjury** |
| **Contents** | Business names, addresses, **location and custodian of records**, officers, **enrollment by grade, number of teachers**, attestation that records are maintained, and that criminal-record summary info was obtained per § 44237 |
| **District reporting** | **None.** "Parents are not required to report to their school district" |
| **Instructional time** | **None specified** for private day schools. *(The tutor route differs: 3 hrs/day × 175 days, 8am–4pm)* |
| **Required subjects** | Instruction **in English**, in "the several branches of study required to be taught in the public schools" — EC § 51210 (grades 1–6) and § 51220 (7–12). Grades 1–6 include **PE at ≥200 minutes per 10 school days** |
| **Assessment** | **None listed** in CDE's requirements or in EC 33190/48222/48224 |
| **Records** | Three mandatory: (1) **attendance in a register**, indicating **every absence of a half day or more**; (2) **courses of study offered**; (3) **names, addresses and educational qualifications of faculty** |
| **Parent qualification** | Persons "**capable of teaching**" — **no credential**. But the PSA requires a record of each faculty member's qualifications. *(Tutor route requires a valid credential.)* |
| **Background checks** | § 44237 fingerprinting **expressly excludes** "a parent or legal guardian working exclusively with his or her children" |
| **Immunization** | `UNVERIFIED` — CDE's mandatory list omits it; Health & Safety Code § 120335 not verified |

**Statute:** Cal. Educ. Code §§ 33190, 48222, 48224, 51210, 51220, 44237
**Sources:** [CDE Schooling at Home, reviewed 2025-09-03](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/ps/homeschool.asp) *(state DOE)* · [CDE Education Code reproduction](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/ps/psaffedcode.asp) *(state DOE reproducing statute)* · [PSA filing](http://www3.cde.ca.gov/psa) *(state DOE)*
**Verified:** 2026-08-30

**⚠ Sourcing note:** leginfo.legislature.ca.gov returned empty bodies. All statutory
text above is CDE's own verbatim reproduction — authoritative-adjacent, one step from
the official code. Worth a hand spot-check.

## Rules for this table going forward

1. `sourceUrl` and `verifiedOn` are **required**. A state without them does not load
2. An **unverified state says so** and falls back to generic record-keeping. It never
   guesses a rule
3. `verifiedOn` older than **12 months** raises a warning. Every state here except
   Florida has been touched within three years — **Georgia five times since 2021**
4. Add states as families need them, not in advance
5. Where a state has multiple legal routes, **name them.** Five of these six do, and
   silently picking one for a family would be the worst failure mode in this file
